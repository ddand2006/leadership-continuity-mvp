# Domain Glossary

## Coaching record

Information created or maintained in connection with a coaching engagement. A coaching record may be private to the coach, shared with the candidate, or shared with the organization.

## Private coach note

A coach-authored reflection or working note that is not part of the shared candidate or organization record. It remains private to the coach unless a separate rule or legal requirement applies.

## Shared coaching record

A coaching plan, agreed action, shared session note, candidate reflection, or organization update that authorized participants may access according to its visibility and sharing permissions.

## Retention policy

The rule that determines how long a record category is retained and what happens when the retention period ends. The policy is governed by the platform administrator and must account for the longest applicable legal, contractual, or legal-hold requirement.

## Legal hold

A recorded instruction that temporarily prevents deletion or anonymization of selected records because they may be needed for a dispute, investigation, audit, or other legal process.

## Termination export

An export of records that authorized users may receive when an organization ends platform use, limited to the records each recipient was permitted to access.

## Needs Review match

A coach recommendation that has useful partial alignment but does not satisfy every preferred matching condition. It is not an approval or eligibility determination.
