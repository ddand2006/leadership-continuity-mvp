# Multi-surface journeys

## Candidate to mentor

Candidate submits or updates a project, mentor reviews it for the relevant role, and the mentor’s view confirms the role-specific record. Proof is captured from the mentor side, not only from the candidate action.

## Candidate to coach

Candidate requests a share, mentor approves the role-specific share, and the coach sees only the explicitly shared item. Revoke the share and verify the coach no longer sees it.

## Administrator to role pipeline

Administrator creates or updates the role composite, assigns a role mentor, and candidate detail reflects the role-specific readiness policy and assignment.

## Partner application to referral payout

A platform operator approves the partner application and configures the manually negotiated commission. The partner owner confirms workspace access and shares the referral link. A referred organization completes an eligible test subscription, then the platform operator verifies the ledger entry and approves the payout after its hold. Proof is captured from the partner payment history and the platform payout queue.

## Partner coach affiliation

A partner administrator adds an existing coach or sends an invitation. The coach accepts the affiliation in Marketplace Readiness. A company user filters the marketplace by partner and confirms the accepted, eligible coach appears. Leaving or declining the roster must remove the affiliation from marketplace results without deleting the coach profile or existing engagements.
