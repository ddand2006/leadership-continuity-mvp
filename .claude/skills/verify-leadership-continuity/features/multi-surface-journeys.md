# Multi-surface journeys

## Candidate to mentor

Candidate submits or updates a project, mentor reviews it for the relevant role, and the mentor’s view confirms the role-specific record. Proof is captured from the mentor side, not only from the candidate action.

## Candidate to coach

Candidate requests a share, mentor approves the role-specific share, and the coach sees only the explicitly shared item. Revoke the share and verify the coach no longer sees it.

## Administrator to role pipeline

Administrator creates or updates the role composite, assigns a role mentor, and candidate detail reflects the role-specific readiness policy and assignment.
